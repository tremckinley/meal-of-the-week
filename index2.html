McKinley Meal Plan

Simple, healthy meals for the week.

Honey-Garlic Sheet Pan Chicken & Broccoli

  1. Preheat your oven to 400°F (200°C).
  2. Cut chicken into bite-sized pieces and broccoli into small florets.
  3. Whisk together 2 tbsp honey, 2 tbsp soy sauce, and 1 clove minced garlic.
  4. Toss everything on a large baking sheet until coated.
  5. Bake for 18-20 minutes until chicken is cooked and broccoli is tender for the toddler.
WFH Tip: You can prep the sauce and chop the chicken during a quick morning break, then just slide the tray in the oven 20 minutes before lunch.

Blackstone Chicken & Zucchini "Hibachi"

  1. Preheat the Blackstone griddle to medium-high.
  2. Dice the chicken, zucchini, and onion.
  3. Sauté chicken with a splash of soy sauce and garlic on one section of the griddle.
  4. On the other side, cook the zucchini "coins" and onions until soft and slightly charred.
  5. Toss together and serve immediately over steamed rice.
Toddler Tip: Zucchini cooks very fast on the griddle; make sure theirs is extra soft so it's easy to chew.

One-Pot Creamy Tomato & Basil Pasta

  1. Add dry pasta, 2 cups chicken broth, and 1 cup tomato sauce to a medium pot.
  2. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat and simmer, stirring occasionally, until pasta is tender (about 10-12 mins).
  3. Stir in a splash of heavy cream or a dollop of Greek yogurt for creaminess.
  4. Chop a handful of fresh basil from your plants and stir it in right before serving.
Note: If the sauce is too thick, add a little more broth. The fresh basil adds great flavor without the heat.

Turkey & Carrot Baked Meatballs

  1. Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. Finely grate the carrots. Mix with ground turkey, 1/4 cup breadcrumbs, and a pinch of salt.
  3. Roll into small, toddler-sized meatballs (about 1 inch).
  4. Place on a greased baking sheet and bake for 15-20 minutes.
  5. Serve with a side of rice or mashed carrots.
Why it works: Unlike sloppy joes, these stay together, making it much easier for a 2-year-old to manage with their fingers or a fork.
