Garlic-Lemon Shrimp & Broccoli

Ingredients

  • 1.5 lbs Shrimp (peeled/deveined)
  • 2 heads Broccoli (small florets)
  • 3 cloves Garlic (minced)
  • 1 Lemon (zested and juiced)
  • 2 tbsp Olive Oil or Ghee

Instructions

  1. Preheat griddle to medium-high.
  2. Toss shrimp and broccoli in a bowl with oil, garlic, and lemon zest.
  3. Spread on the griddle. Cook shrimp 2-3 mins per side until pink.
  4. Sauté broccoli until tender-crisp (about 5-6 mins).
  5. Drizzle with lemon juice before serving.
Toddler Tip: Cut the shrimp into pea-sized bites and ensure the broccoli florets are soft enough to mash.

Ground Turkey & Sweet Potato Skillet

Ingredients

  • 1.5 lbs Ground Turkey
  • 2 Sweet Potatoes (small dice)
  • 1 Bell Pepper (diced)
  • 1/2 Onion (finely chopped)
  • 1/2 tsp Garlic Powder & Salt

Instructions

  1. Sauté sweet potatoes in a skillet with oil/ghee until they start to soften (8-10 mins).
  2. Add onions and peppers, cooking until translucent.
  3. Add ground turkey and seasonings.
  4. Cook until turkey is browned and sweet potatoes are fork-tender.
WFH Tip: Dice the sweet potatoes very small (1/4 inch) to cut the cooking time in half.

Beef & Peppers with Roasted Potatoes

Ingredients

  • 1.5 lbs Ground Beef
  • 2 Bell Peppers (sliced)
  • 1.5 lbs Potatoes (cubed)
  • 1/2 Onion (sliced)
  • Olive Oil, Salt, Pepper

Instructions

  1. Toss potato cubes in oil and salt. Roast at 400°F for 25-30 mins.
  2. While potatoes roast, brown ground beef in a skillet.
  3. Add onions and peppers to the beef and sauté until soft.
  4. Serve the beef/pepper mix over or alongside the roasted potatoes.
Note: Ensure your oil is compliant (no soybean or canola oil).

Lemon-Herb Roasted Chicken & Carrots

Ingredients

  • 2 lbs Chicken Thighs
  • 1 lb Carrots (thick coins)
  • 1 Lemon (sliced)
  • 1 tbsp Dried Oregano & Thyme
  • 3 tbsp Olive Oil

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 400°F.
  2. Place chicken and carrots on a large sheet pan.
  3. Drizzle with oil and rub in the herbs, salt, and pepper.
  4. Top chicken with lemon slices.
  5. Roast for 35-40 mins until chicken is 165°F and carrots are very soft.
Toddler Tip: Roasted carrots become very sweet and soft, making them a perfect side for pickier eaters.
