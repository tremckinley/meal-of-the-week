Blackstone BBQ Chicken & Sweet Corn

Ingredients

  • 0.75 lbs Chicken Breast (diced)
  • 1/2 cup Mild BBQ Sauce
  • 1 cup Sweet Corn (frozen or canned)
  • 1 Red Bell Pepper (diced)

Instructions

  1. Dice chicken breast into small pieces and toss with the BBQ sauce.
  2. Preheat the griddle to medium-high.
  3. Sauté the chicken on one side and the corn and diced peppers on the other.
  4. Cook until chicken is done and vegetables are slightly charred but tender (6-8 mins).
  5. Mix together and serve.
Cooking Tip: The sauce can caramelize quickly on the griddle, so keep the food moving for an even sear.

Mild Turkey & White Bean "White Chili"

Ingredients

  • 1 lb Ground Turkey
  • 1 can (15 oz) White Beans (rinsed)
  • 2 cups Chicken Broth
  • 1/2 tsp Cumin & 1/2 tsp Garlic Powder

Instructions

  1. Brown the ground turkey in a large pot over medium heat.
  2. Add rinsed white beans, chicken broth, and spices.
  3. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low and simmer for 15-20 minutes.
  4. Mash a few of the beans against the side of the pot to thicken the chili.
Texture Tip: Mashing the beans makes the consistency easier for a child to handle with a spoon.

One-Pot Cheesy Beef and Shells with Diced Carrots

Ingredients

  • 1 lb Ground Beef
  • 2 cups Pasta Shells (dry)
  • 2 cups Beef or Chicken Broth
  • 2-3 Carrots (very finely diced)
  • 1 cup Shredded Cheddar Cheese

Instructions

  1. Brown the ground beef and drain any excess fat.
  2. Add pasta shells, broth, and very finely diced carrots to the pot.
  3. Simmer until pasta is tender and most liquid is absorbed (about 10-12 mins).
  4. Remove from heat and stir in shredded cheddar cheese until melted and creamy.
Prep Tip: Dice the carrots as small as possible so they cook in the same amount of time as the pasta.

Baked White Fish with "Buttery" Smashed Peas

Ingredients

  • 1 lb White Fish Fillets (Cod or Tilapia)
  • 1/2 Lemon (juiced)
  • 1 tbsp Butter
  • 1 cup Frozen Peas

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C). Season fish with lemon juice and a pat of butter.
  2. Bake for 12-15 minutes until the fish flakes easily with a fork.
  3. Boil frozen peas for 3 minutes, drain, and smash them lightly with a fork and a little butter.
  4. Break the fish into small pieces and serve alongside the smashed peas.
Safety Tip: Always double-check white fish for any small bones before serving.
