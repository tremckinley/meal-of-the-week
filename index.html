Lemon Garlic Chicken Breast & Asparagus

A fast, high-heat Blackstone griddle meal that keeps cooking heat entirely outside [cite: User Summary].

Ingredients

  • 2 lbs chicken breasts (thinly sliced cutlets) [cite: User Summary]
  • 2 bunches asparagus spears (tough ends trimmed) [cite: User Summary]
  • 3 tbsp avocado oil [cite: User Summary]
  • 1 head garlic (cloves minced)
  • 1 fresh lime (juiced)
  • Salt, pepper, garlic powder

Instructions

  1. Preheat your outdoor griddle to medium-high heat [cite: User Summary].
  2. Season chicken cutlets generously with avocado oil, salt, pepper, and garlic powder.
  3. Toss asparagus spears in avocado oil, minced garlic, and salt.
  4. Lay chicken cutlets flat on one side of the griddle. Cook for 3–4 minutes per side until internal temperature hits 165°F.
  5. Simultaneously, drop asparagus onto the other side of the griddle. Toss frequently, then trap under a melting dome with a tiny splash of water for 2–3 minutes to rapidly steam-soften them.
  6. Remove everything, drizzle chicken with fresh lime juice, and let rest.

💻 WFH Efficiency Tip

Buy pre-sliced chicken cutlets. Griddle setup to plate takes under 15 minutes flat, giving you an actual break during your lunch block.

👶 Toddler Safety & Texture

The griddle dome ensures the asparagus gets fork-tender rather than rubbery. Cut chicken across the grain into tiny, easily chewable cubes for Mason [cite: User Summary].

Beef & Bacon Burgers with Sweet Potato Fries

A simple griddle dinner paired with a hands-free, soft baked side [cite: User Summary].

Ingredients

  • 1.5 lbs ground beef (80/20 mix) [cite: User Summary]
  • 1 pack sugar-free, Whole30-compliant bacon
  • 3 large sweet potatoes (peeled and cut into wedges) [cite: User Summary]
  • 2 tbsp avocado oil [cite: User Summary]
  • Salt, pepper, garlic powder

Instructions

  1. Preheat your indoor kitchen oven to 400°F for the fries. Firing up the outdoor griddle to medium-high [cite: User Summary].
  2. Toss sweet potato wedges with avocado oil, salt, and garlic powder. Spread evenly across a baking sheet and bake for 25–30 minutes until soft inside.
  3. Shape ground beef into round patties, seasoning only with salt and pepper [cite: User Summary].
  4. Lay bacon strips on the griddle first to render down fat. Move them over when crispy.
  5. Sear the beef patties directly in the bacon fat, cooking 3–4 minutes per side until fully done. Serve wrapped in plain lettuce leaves or on a plate cleanly [cite: User Summary].

💻 WFH Efficiency Tip

Drop the sweet potatoes in the oven right before your final wrap-up meeting of the day. By the time you drop off the call, the oven work is done and the griddle takes minutes.

👶 Toddler Safety & Texture

Peeled, thick-cut roasted sweet potato wedges become incredibly soft inside. Mash them slightly with a fork on Mason's plate so they aren't a choking risk [cite: User Summary].

Sheet Pan Garlic Herb Salmon & Summer Squash

A completely hands-off oven meal that requires zero oversight while baking.

Ingredients

  • 3 center-cut salmon fillets (approx. 1.25 lbs)
  • 2 medium yellow squash (sliced into thin rounds) [cite: User Summary]
  • 2 medium zucchini (sliced into thin rounds) [cite: User Summary]
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tbsp dried thyme & oregano mix
  • Salt, pepper, garlic powder

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 400°F. Line a large sheet pan with parchment paper.
  2. Arrange salmon fillets in the center of the pan. Brush with olive oil and season with salt, pepper, and garlic powder.
  3. Toss squash and zucchini rounds with olive oil, dried herbs, and salt [cite: User Summary]. Scatter them thickly all around the salmon.
  4. Roast for 12–15 minutes until the salmon easily flakes with a fork and the squash rounds are totally tender.

💻 WFH Efficiency Tip

Zero active monitoring needed here. Pop the sheet pan in the oven, walk away, and handle your tasks. Minimal cleanup since it's a single pan.

👶 Toddler Safety & Texture

Salmon flakes perfectly into soft, manageable textures. The summer squash becomes completely melt-in-your-mouth tender when baked, making it simple for a toddler to manage [cite: User Summary].

One-Pot Creamy Coconut Lime Chicken Thighs & Spinach

A rich, tropical-flavored dinner done in one skillet that keeps chicken moist and tender [cite: User Summary].

Ingredients

  • 1.5 lbs boneless, skinless chicken thighs (cut into bite-sized pieces) [cite: User Summary]
  • 1 can full-fat unsweetened coconut milk [cite: User Summary]
  • 1 large bag (5oz) baby spinach [cite: User Summary]
  • 2 fresh limes (juiced)
  • 1 tbsp avocado oil [cite: User Summary]
  • Salt, pepper, garlic powder

Instructions

  1. Heat avocado oil over medium heat in a deep skillet or Dutch oven [cite: User Summary].
  2. Add the diced chicken thighs, seasoning with salt, pepper, and garlic powder [cite: User Summary]. Sauté until lightly browned (about 6–8 minutes).
  3. Pour in the full can of full-fat coconut milk and bring to a light simmer [cite: User Summary]. Turn down heat to low, cover, and let simmer for 10 minutes to tenderize meat.
  4. Remove the lid, stir in the fresh lime juice, and dump the baby spinach on top [cite: User Summary]. Stir constantly for 1–2 minutes until the spinach wilts completely into the sauce.

💻 WFH Efficiency Tip

Cutting the chicken into bite-sized pieces *before* cooking cuts total stove time in half. Leftovers reheat beautifully without drying out for lunch tomorrow.

👶 Toddler Safety & Texture

Chicken thighs contain more moisture than breasts and don't get dry or tough [cite: User Summary]. The spinach wilts down completely into a soft sauce, eliminating stringy choking hazards [cite: User Summary].
